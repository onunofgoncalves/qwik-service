Government safety inspection

Vehicles must pass a periodic roadworthiness inspection to stay legally on the road. Bring your registration document and, if your vehicle has been inspected before, the previous certificate.

Not every workshop is authorised to carry one out. Ask on the call and we will only offer you dates at a certified centre.

Before you call

What do I need to have ready?

Your licence plate and the postal code on your account. If you are calling from a number we do not recognise, we will also ask for the phone number registered to the account.

What time should I arrive for my service?

Arrive about 10 to 15 minutes early so check-in is quick and easy.

Will I get a confirmation email or SMS?

Yes. A confirmation is typically sent by email, and optional text reminders may be available.

Can I change my service centre?

You can usually change locations before final confirmation, though you may need to reselect available services or times.

Are loaner vehicles available during service?

Loaner availability varies by location and service type. Check with your service centre when booking.

What if I am not sure what my vehicle needs?

Book a general inspection or diagnostic visit and discuss the symptoms with the technician.
